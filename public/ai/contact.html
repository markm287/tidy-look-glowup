What to Expect

Your initial consultation at Radiance is $50, which is fully credited toward any service booked at or after your visit. This fee ensures your provider's time is reserved exclusively for you.

Cancellation policy: Please cancel or reschedule any appointment at least 48 hours in advance. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before your appointment incur a $50 fee.

During your consultation — typically 30–45 minutes — one of our providers will review your concerns, discuss your goals, and build a personalized treatment plan. You are never obligated to book a treatment on the same day.
