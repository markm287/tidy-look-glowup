Package terms

Consultation & payment

All packages require an initial provider consultation and, where needed, clinical assessment. Your provider may decline or modify a series based on medical history, contraindications, or clinical judgment. Treatment-specific consent is required before the first session. Pay in full at purchase or through a payment plan offered at our discretion; payment must be complete before the final session. Your purchased package price is locked in.

Validity & appointments

Unless agreed otherwise in writing at purchase, complete all sessions within 18 months of purchase. Unused sessions expire and are not refundable except for an approved medical exception. Schedule sessions at the interval your provider recommends. Keep a card on file and give at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations or rescheduling within 48 hours forfeit the session’s deposit or hold and require a new deposit to reschedule. A no-show or late cancellation forfeits the session’s hold and may be counted as a used session at our discretion. Documented emergencies may be reviewed by management.

Refunds & medical pauses

Before the first session, you may cancel for a refund of amounts paid minus a 10% processing fee. After the first session, packages are non-refundable except as required by Pennsylvania law or approved under a medical exception. An approved partial refund is calculated after deducting used sessions at then-current single-session prices. For pregnancy, documented medical conditions, or a provider-determined contraindication, request a pause in writing before expiry; the validity period may extend by the documented interruption, up to 12 additional months.

Transfers & other offers

Packages have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for unrelated services or retail. Unused sessions may be transferred once to an immediate family member with written request and management approval; treatment types cannot be substituted without a case-by-case review. VIP package pricing is 20% off regular single-session totals and does not stack with tier discounts or other promotions unless a promotion expressly allows it. Cancelling VIP does not shorten a purchased package’s validity.

Changes to your package

If Radiance closes, we may offer a refund of unused value or a comparable provider referral or credit arrangement. If a provider leaves, another qualified Radiance provider will honor remaining sessions. The terms in effect when you purchase govern your package; later policy updates do not change it unless required by law or more favorable to you. Questions? contact@radiancepa.com.
